         Our approach​
         Driving meaningful sustainability progress requires choosing the right intervention for the
         problem at hand. Some challenges are best addressed by reducing demand at the source,
         while others require helping solutions reach commercial scale. Still others depend on the
         policies, infrastructure, or local partnerships that make progress possible.​
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/topics/sustainability/report/                             4/18

         Key levers that advance sustainability
         progress ​
         Across our sustainability work, four key levers help us accelerate progress: improving
         efficiency, building markets, advancing policy, and forging partnerships.​




         Improving efficiency​
         Reducing environmental impact at the source by designing infrastructure, products, and
         operations to use energy, water, land, and materials more efficiently.​


         Advancing policy​
         Engaging with policymakers and industry stakeholders to help create the market conditions
         that enable sustainability solutions to scale.​


         Building markets​
         Accelerating emerging sustainability solutions by using investment, procurement, and long-
         term demand to help scale availability.​


         Forging partnerships​
         Engaging with policymakers and industry stakeholders to help create the market conditions
         that enable sustainability solutions to scale.​

         Infrastructure
         Our digital infrastructure tools are powered by a growing network of physical infrastructure:
         datacenters, campuses, supply chains, and energy systems. As we expand capacity, these
         systems are where many of our sustainability commitments are put into practice—from
         reducing electricity, water, materials, and emissions impacts to working with partners on
         carbon-free electricity, grid resilience, value chain emissions, and carbon removal.​

            Software and platforms​
            We’re building software and platforms that make sustainability more accessible and
            actionable for the people and organizations using them, from tools that help
            organizations track and reduce emissions to features that lower the energy impact of
            over a billion Windows devices.​



            XBOX​
            We’re advancing gaming sustainability by reducing the energy footprint of XBOX devices,
            and using the games themselves to engage players around the world in environmental
            action.​



            Applying AI for sustainability​
            We’re using AI to help address environmental challenges, strengthening water resilience,
            supporting ecosystem conservation, and improving access to climate data. ​



            Devices​
            We’re working to reduce the environmental impact of our physical devices across their
            full life cycle, from using recycled and lower-carbon materials to improving repairability,
            reducing manufacturing energy use, and applying AI to better understand product
            impact.​



            Logistics and supply chain​​
            We’re improving the efficiency of our own logistics network, helping suppliers advance
            their decarbonization pathways, and working with partners to strengthen the
